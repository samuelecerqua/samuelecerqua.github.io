Orbital Mechanics, Mission Design (Team)
31/30 with honors
Politecnico di Milano
Designed an interplanetary mission from Mercury to Asteroid 290999 under an Earth-flyby constraint and 2030–2060 launch, flyby and arrival windows, a 3D optimization problem where brute-force minimization over the temporal discretization is out of reach for normal PCs. Solved with Particle Swarm Optimization plus a custom strategy that intercepts the pseudo-pattern in the projection of the objects' spatial coordinates at coarse discretization, isolating a specific 3D temporal zone and refining the time discretization there.
Also designed a planetary mission around Earth, modelling second-order disturbances (third-body effects from the Moon, non-sphericity of Earth) and a Repeating Ground Track, followed by analysis of unfiltered real TLE data for two uncontrolled objects (“METEOR 2-6 DE”, “COSMOS 862”). Output: a 15-page LaTeX formal report.
MATLAB Astrodynamics PSO TLE Analysis LaTeX
